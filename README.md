## What this repository is

This repository documents **Encuentro Nacional 4/20²⁶ Pro-Legalización 🌿** as a **replicable cultural celebration of free entry and free participation**.

It is rooted in Bolivia, with **Proyecto Cultural Barranco** as the documented 2026 reference case. It is designed to be useful beyond a single space, city, or edition — open to future adaptations for other territories, languages, and legal frameworks if enough interest and stewardship emerge.

The goal is not only to document a past event, but to leave an open structure that other host spaces, collaborators, and communities can understand, adapt, and replicate.

## Core idea

The 4/20²⁶ at Barranco showed something deeper than a cannabis advocacy event. Cannabis legalization was the shared goal — but the deeper center was the alternative way of organizing: co-creating, adapting, caring for the space, collaborating, and being together as a diverse community.

**Voluntariado Barranco** seeks to cultivate that same possibility throughout the year: a freer, more responsible, and more human way of living, organizing, and creating together.

We believe that open, well-cared-for, and increasingly visible cultural celebrations can help reduce stigma, invite skeptics and the general public into a more mature conversation, and gradually bring Bolivia closer to a serious discussion about cannabis legalization. The main strategy is not confrontation, but social visibility through example, hospitality, culture, and shared experience.

## The two pillars toward 4/20²⁷

### Strengthening the Manual 4/20 🌿

The [Manual 4/20 🌿](https://manual420.barranco.life) is the living resource for context, harm reduction, and shared learning. The goal is to make it more complete, more socially readable, and eventually printable — reaching universities, cultural spaces, homes, and public conversations.

### Replication: how and why to organize a 4/20

Replicating 4/20 events across different cities is not a logistical detail: it is part of the movement's strategy. Every space that opens helps make the community more visible, reduce stigma, and turn legalization into a conversation that becomes harder to ignore.
